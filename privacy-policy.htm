Mayoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Mayoon (Android package com.pirhaan.mayoon) is a family app. This policy explains what the app stores and what it does not do.

What the app stores

On the phone only: sign-in details, PINs, pictures Uncle uploads, trivia Uncle adds, and messages Mayoon writes.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an online account.
  • It does not send pictures, messages, or passwords to a server.
  • It does not use ads, analytics, or trackers.
  • It does not sell data.
  • It does not request your location.

Photos

When Uncle picks a photo, a copy is saved inside the app’s private folder on that device. The original photo stays in the gallery.

Children

The app is meant for a child in your family, with an adult (Uncle) who controls sign-in and uploads. Do not share the access code publicly.

Deleting data

Uncle can delete photos and messages in the app. You can also clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, which removes the local copies.

Contact

Questions about this app: use the email address listed on the Play Store listing.
